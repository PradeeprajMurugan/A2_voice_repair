Sous

A kitchen voice assistant you can interrupt when it gets things wrong.

Behind the scenes →

The problem: voice assistants mishear. When your hands are covered in dough, fixing the mistake is the hard part — you can't tap a screen, and most assistants make you wait, then start again.

Sous's answer: just talk over it. It stops, undoes the mistake, says what it undid, and fixes it — asking only for what it missed. Try the three short stories below.
